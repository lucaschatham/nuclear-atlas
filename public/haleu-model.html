Assumptions, formulas & sources
Snapshot, not live synchronization

Fuel-demand and core-cost model snapshot, September 16, 2026. This standalone calculator uses the accounting and references documented below. Inputs are illustrative planning assumptions.

Presentation starts at 50 cores/year. Use presets to explore 20 to 52,560 initial cores per year. Defaults are 500 kgU/core and a hypothetical $300/SWU single rate. “Reset” restores presentation defaults.

Fuel basis remains illustrative

500 kgU means uranium mass, not compound or finished-fuel mass. The rounded “20%” assumption is treated as rounded to 19.75%. Initial cores only unless annual reload kgU is explicitly entered. No dated deployment ramp, losses or inventory buffer. No design approval or supplier qualification is implied.

Pricing evidence

$180/upstream SWU and $55/kgU conversion are legacy workbook inputs, not verified offers. $1,099.37/final-stage SWU is the 2023 NIA $1,000 assumption multiplied by 334.980 / 304.702 CPI. Uranium: $96.50/lb U₃O₈, attributed in the workbook to Cameco’s August 31, 2026 indicator. $300 is a hypothetical single rate.

Capacity comparisons

Urenco results: planned initial 27 tU/year HALEU, early 2030s. Potential doubling: 54 tU/year, demand-dependent. Centrus planned initial buildout: 12 tU/year; first new capacity expected 2029, full ramp unspecified.

Broad market & feed context

Workbook denominators: Urenco + Orano 24.7m SWU/year; global 2025 capacity 68.076m; US 2025 purchased enrichment 12.710m. Global includes Russia and China. These are dated, non-interchangeable comparisons. NEA/IAEA 2024 mine output: 61,924 tU.

Commercial limits

No volume-discount curve, binding purchase obligation, supplier allocation, contribution margin or financing concession has been supplied. The DOE $17,000/kgU comparator bundles material and other obligations with separate capacity support. It is not an enrichment-only or finished-fuel quotation. Comparisons are carried from the workbook; no new market verification was performed for this conversion.

Accounting used in the workbook

V(x) = (1 − 2x) × ln((1 − x) / x) F = (product assay − tails assay) / (feed assay − tails assay) SWU/kgU = V(product) + (F − 1) × V(tails) − F × V(feed) Final-stage feed ratio = (product − 0.00711) / (0.0495 − 0.00711) Final-stage SWU = V(product) + (ratio − 1) × V(0.00711) − ratio × V(0.0495) Upstream SWU = total SWU − final-stage SWU Front-end $/kgU = F × (2.599776385057202 × $/lb U₃O₈ + conversion $/kgU feed) + enrichment $/kgU Annual kgU = initial cores/year × core kgU + annual reload kgU Capital recovery factor = r / (1 − (1 + r)^(−years)); at r = 0, use 1/years

Higher-stage tails receive full recycle credit at natural assay. Contractual ownership and treatment require confirmation. The model represents material and work accounting, not a plant configuration.

# IAEA-based stakeholder roles and dashboard questions

Basis: [IAEA NG-G-5.1 (2021)](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/publications/PDF/PUB1967_web.pdf), Figure 1 and Section 4. The framework is non-exhaustive. Person labels, role specializations, and questions are Atlas adaptations, not official IAEA personas or validated question rankings. Source of truth: data/personas.json.

All previous question-level capability ratings are withdrawn pending a record-and-field audit. No new dashboard controls are implemented. The stakeholder map includes groups whose questions exceed current U.S.-focused coverage. Tribal governments require a jurisdiction-specific extension; they must not be treated as interchangeable with community commenters.

## Community Member

Lives near a proposed or existing nuclear facility

IAEA category: Local community; Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- What is proposed near me, exactly where, and who is responsible?
- Is this an announcement, a license application, an approved project, or construction?
- What does the environmental review say about water, land use, and construction effects?
- What spent fuel or waste would remain at the site, and what plan is documented?
- When can I attend a public meeting, submit comments, or contact the responsible authority?

Candidate filters: Location, Project stage, Public notice date.

## Government Official

A local, regional, or national official involved in nuclear decisions

IAEA category: Government; Figure 1; Section 4.1.

- Which projects and facilities fall within our jurisdiction?
- Which decisions belong to local, regional, or national authorities?
- What milestones or public consultations require our participation?
- What local infrastructure, employment, and environmental effects are documented?
- What responsibilities remain after operations end?

Candidate filters: Jurisdiction, Responsible authority, Decision milestone.

## Regulatory Reviewer

Reviews nuclear applications, compliance, or oversight records

IAEA category: Regulatory body; Section 4.2.

- Which applications and license actions are publicly recorded for a facility?
- Who is the applicant or license holder?
- What is the documented stage of the regulatory review?
- Which source documents support the reported status and dates?
- Which related facilities or activities need to be distinguished from this application?

Candidate filters: Docket, License type, Regulatory milestone.

## Project Developer

Advances a proposed nuclear facility through development

IAEA category: Owner/operator; Section 4.3; Atlas specialization.

- Which comparable projects use the technology we are evaluating?
- Which projects have progressed from announcement to an application or permit?
- What site and environmental review milestones are documented?
- Which fuel or electricity purchase commitments have been publicly disclosed?
- What supports the reported construction and operating dates?

Candidate filters: Technology, Development stage, Target date.

## Facility Owner or Operator

Owns or operates a reactor or fuel-cycle facility

IAEA category: Owner/operator; Section 4.3.

- Which public records describe this facility and its operating status?
- What generation, capacity, and outage information is publicly available?
- Which license changes or renewals have been documented?
- What fuel-cycle and spent-fuel facilities are associated with the site?
- What closure or decommissioning milestones are publicly reported?

Candidate filters: Facility type, Operating status, License date.

## Fuel Buyer

Procures fuel or fuel-cycle services for a developer or operator

IAEA category: Owner/operator; Section 4.3; Atlas specialization.

- Which organizations report uranium supply, conversion, enrichment, or fabrication capabilities?
- Which suppliers report producing TRISO fuel, and where are their facilities?
- Which suppliers handle low-enriched uranium or HALEU, and at which processing step?
- What operating status and licensed or announced capacity are documented?
- What must we confirm directly about qualification, available supply, price, and delivery?

Candidate filters: Fuel form, Enrichment category, Supply-chain step.

## Supplier or Contractor

Provides fuel, equipment, engineering, construction, or services

IAEA category: Suppliers; Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- Which projects could need the capability or service we provide?
- Which organizations are developing or operating those projects?
- What development and licensing milestones have been reached?
- What procurement requirements or supplier announcements are public?
- Which target dates are supported by evidence rather than an announcement alone?

Candidate filters: Project stage, Facility type, Required capability.

## Electricity Buyer

Evaluates nuclear electricity for a utility or business

IAEA category: Business and industry; Figure 1; Atlas specialization.

- Which nuclear projects have announced electricity purchase agreements?
- Who are the named buyers, developers, and operators?
- Does reported capacity refer to the whole plant or a particular agreement?
- What delivery date is reported, and which milestones support it?
- Which commercial terms and remaining supply require direct confirmation?

Candidate filters: Buyer, Commitment type, Capacity basis.

## Nuclear Worker

Works in nuclear construction, operations, or site closure

IAEA category: Employees; Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- Which nearby facilities are being built, operated, or decommissioned?
- Who is responsible for the site and its major contracts?
- What project milestones could affect the timing of work?
- What workforce or training requirements have been publicly documented?
- What closure or transition plans have been announced for the site?

Candidate filters: Site, Lifecycle stage, Workforce milestone.

## Union Representative

Represents workers affected by nuclear development or closure

IAEA category: Workers’ unions; Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- Which projects could create, extend, or end work for our members?
- Which employers and contractors are publicly identified?
- What workforce commitments appear in public project documents?
- What construction or closure schedule underlies those commitments?
- Where are opportunities to participate in decisions affecting workers?

Candidate filters: Employer, Project milestone, Workforce commitment.

## Researcher or Technical Expert

Studies nuclear systems, policy, or impacts

IAEA category: Scientific community; Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- Which original sources support this record’s claims?
- What dates, units, and geographic boundaries apply to the data?
- Which records can be compared on a consistent basis?
- Where are missing values, conflicting claims, or approximate locations?
- Can I reproduce the published snapshot and cite its sources?

Candidate filters: Source date, Evidence type, Coverage.

## University Educator or Student

Teaches or learns about the nuclear lifecycle

IAEA category: Universities; Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- Which real facilities illustrate each stage of the nuclear lifecycle?
- How do fuel production, electricity generation, and spent-fuel storage differ?
- What does a license or public filing establish about a project?
- How can I distinguish an exact site location from an approximate marker?
- Which original documents can I use for further study?

Candidate filters: Lifecycle stage, Facility type, Source.

## Journalist

Reports on nuclear projects, policy, or local impacts

IAEA category: Media; Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- What has actually happened, and what is still only proposed?
- Which original document supports the headline claim?
- Who owns, develops, operates, or regulates the facility?
- What is unknown, disputed, or out of date?
- What upcoming public decisions or meetings warrant follow-up?

Candidate filters: Announcement date, Evidence strength, Responsible organization.

## Civil-Society Representative

Represents an environmental or other public-interest organization

IAEA category: NGOs; Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- Which proposals or facilities affect the interests we represent?
- What environmental and waste-management claims are documented?
- What evidence supports claimed benefits and potential burdens?
- Which organizations hold responsibility for the relevant decisions?
- When and how can our organization participate or submit evidence?

Candidate filters: Environmental review, Public notice, Waste activity.

## Neighbouring-Country Representative

Works on nuclear developments with cross-border relevance

IAEA category: International community (including neighbouring countries); Figure 1, printed p. 6.

- Which facilities or proposals have potential cross-border relevance?
- Which national authorities are responsible?
- What environmental assessments address effects beyond the host jurisdiction?
- What public consultation or cooperation arrangements are documented?
- Where do differences in definitions, dates, or reporting limit comparisons?

Candidate filters: Country, Border region, Responsible authority.

## Before implementing filters

Map each question to available records and required fields. Verify units, dates, completeness, and unknown handling. Interview role representatives to establish priorities. Keep fuel form and enrichment category separate. Public capacity is not available supply. Categories identify stakeholders, not proven Atlas customers.

## Title

What should Nuclear Atlas answer?

## Description

**Nuclear Atlas** is an open-source dashboard that makes fragmented public nuclear information easier to find, compare, and understand.

I'm building it so people across the nuclear industry can answer important questions without hunting through PDFs and disconnected databases.

Your <60-second response will help decide which questions and data sources I build first.

Responses stay private. Optional public research-contributor credit is opt-in. Please don't share confidential or security-sensitive information.

## Questions

### 1. Your role?

Multiple choice. Required.

- Operations / safety / licensing
- Development / construction / decommissioning
- Fuel / waste / equipment / services
- Outage work / workforce planning
- Energy buying / grid planning
- Finance / market research
- Government / regulation / research
- Community / local business / media
- Other

### 2. Which questions do you need answered repeatedly? Pick up to two.

Checkboxes. Required. Maximum two selections.

- Where are reactors, by type and operating status?
- Which projects are progressing, delayed, licensed, or restarting?
- Which safety risks, studies, and modifications apply at each reactor?
- Which safety lessons may be relevant to similar reactors or sites?
- Where and when are refueling outages and outage jobs?
- How many outage workers visit, for how long, and with what local spending?
- How much electricity does each reactor generate over time?
- Which grid region does each reactor support?
- Where is spent fuel stored, and is it moving offsite?
- Where does each type of radioactive waste go, and by what route?
- What stage, timeline, and obligations govern each decommissioning site?
- How do nuclear plants affect local electricity rates?
- Where are nuclear workforce shortages and retirement risks emerging?
- None of these (select alone)

### 3. What question is missing? (Optional)

Short answer. Optional.

### 4. Want public research-contributor credit? (Optional)

Short answer. Optional.

## Owner setup notes

- One page; only role and voting are required. No email collection or sign-in requirement.
- Keep responses private. Only explicitly opted-in names may enter public acknowledgments.
- Offer the same credit for critical, positive, or "none" responses. Credit is not endorsement, affiliation, or code authorship.
- No prototype visit required. Put the project link in the invitation, not another survey instruction.
- Validate the two-choice limit; check "none" combinations when reviewing responses.
- Time the two required questions on mobile with three people before using the under-a-minute claim. Optional typing may take longer. Shorten the ballot if needed.
- Confirmation: "Thanks. You're helping choose what Nuclear Atlas builds next."
- Copy and test the responder link before sending. Never send the editor URL. No outreach or publication has been performed.

## Decision rule

Compare votes within roles and themes: reactor status, projects and licensing, safety, outages, operations, waste, decommissioning, rates, and workforce. This short survey identifies interest, not demand, frequency, or willingness to pay. Use follow-up conversations with existing contacts to verify concrete work problems before building.
